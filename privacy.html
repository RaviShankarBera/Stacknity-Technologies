Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Stacknity Technologies ("Stacknity Technologies", "we", "us") is a services company under the Stacknity group, based in New Delhi, India. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our client accounts, why we collect it, and how you can reach us about it. You can contact us any time at Stacknity.Technologies@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we collect your name and email address. Your password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider - we never see or store it in plain text.
  • Contact information. When you email us or use a contact form we receive your name, email address, company and the details you choose to share about your project.
  • Usage information. Standard hosting logs such as pages visited, browser and device type, and approximate region, used to keep the site secure and improve it.

2. How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account and sign you in.
  • To respond to enquiries and deliver our services.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent abuse.
  • To improve the website and our services.
  • To meet legal obligations where required.

3. Authentication

Sign-in on this website is powered by Supabase Auth. When you sign in, a session token is stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. You can clear it any time by signing out or clearing your browser data.

4. Cookies and tracking

We do not run advertising trackers. Storage on your device is limited to what is needed to operate the site and keep you signed in.

5. Sharing

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that operate this website (hosting and authentication), who process it under their own safeguards, or when the law requires it.

6. Retention

Account information is kept while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise the associated information, except where we must keep it by law.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your personal information at any time by emailing Stacknity.Technologies@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, passwords are stored only as hashes, and access to systems is limited. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

10. International visitors

We are based in India. Your information may be processed in India and in other countries where our service providers operate.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version here with an updated date.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: Stacknity.Technologies@gmail.com - Stacknity Technologies, New Delhi, India.
